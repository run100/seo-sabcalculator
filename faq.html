FAQ

Full answers about the SAB Calculator trade checker: W/F/L results, income and value formulas, mutations, traits, N/A values, update timing, and account safety.

This FAQ explains how SAB Calculator helps you check Steal a Brainrot trades before you accept. The numbers you see here are community references for comparing offers — not official prices and not a guarantee of what another player will take.

What is Steal a Brainrot?

Steal a Brainrot is a Roblox game where players collect Brainrots, grow income, and trade with each other. Different Brainrots have different earnings rates, rarities, mutations, and traits, so two trades that look similar at a glance can be very different once you compare the details.

SAB Calculator is an independent trade-checking site built for that moment before you click accept. It helps you put both sides of a deal next to each other, apply the correct mutation and traits, and review value, income, and a quick W/F/L result.

How does the trade calculator work?

Start on the calculator homepage. Add the Brainrots you would give on the Offer side, then add the Brainrots you would receive on the Receive side. For each item, open the configuration panel and choose the mutation and traits that match the real trade window in Roblox. You can also raise the quantity when a side includes more than one copy of the same setup.

After both sides are filled in, the calculator totals estimated value and income for each side and shows a W/F/L check. Use that result as a fast second opinion. Always re-read the final Roblox trade screen yourself before accepting, because the live window is what actually transfers.

What does W/F/L mean?

W/F/L means Win, Fair, or Lose. A Win result means the Brainrots you would receive are estimated to be worth more than what you offer. Fair means both sides are close. Lose means your offer may be worth more than what you get back.

W/F/L is a quick balance signal, not a rule you must follow. A trade can still make sense for you if you care more about income, rarity, a specific mutation, or a Brainrot you personally want — even when the value result looks slightly against you.

How are income values calculated?

Income starts from each Brainrot's tracked base earnings rate. The calculator then applies the mutation and trait setup you selected. On SAB Calculator, the income view follows this idea: Base × (Mutation + Traits). Additive trait bonuses are stacked with the mutation multiplier, then any multiplicative trait effects are applied.

Both sides of the trade are totaled separately, so you can see which offer earns more over time. That is useful when two trades look close in value but one side produces clearly stronger income.

How are Brainrot trade values determined?

Trade values on SAB Calculator are community estimates compiled from trading references and related sources. They can move when demand changes, when a game update lands, when a mutation becomes more common, or when players chase a different set of Brainrots.

Think of value as a shared reference for comparing offers, not as an official price tag. If a deal feels off, check the mutation and traits again, compare income as well as value, and look at rarity or exist-count context before you accept.

What does the + next to a value mean?

When the calculator shows a value with a trailing +, it means the displayed figure is an estimated community value for that configured Brainrot, not a fixed in-game store price. The plus mark is a reminder that trading values are approximate and can shift.

If a side shows N/A instead of a number with +, that Brainrot currently has no usable value estimate in the calculator data. Income may still be available for comparison even when value is missing.

Why does a value show N/A?

N/A usually means the calculator does not have a reliable current value for that Brainrot setup. This can happen after a new release, when a mutation setup is incomplete, or when the latest sync has not picked up a fresh estimate yet.

Treat any W/F/L result that includes N/A as incomplete. You can still compare income and the rest of the trade details, but you should not treat the value total as final until the missing estimate is available again.

What are mutations and how do they affect value?

Mutations change how a Brainrot is configured. The same Brainrot name can produce very different income and trade-value results when one copy has a stronger mutation than another. That is why looking only at the name is one of the most common trading mistakes.

Before you trust the calculator result, open each item and set the mutation that appears in the actual trade. If you leave the wrong mutation selected, the W/F/L check can look fair when the real deal is not.

What are traits and how are they valued?

Traits add extra bonuses on top of the selected mutation. On SAB Calculator, trait effects can change both income and estimated trade value. Leaving traits blank when the trade includes them will understate that side and can flip a Fair result into a misleading Win or Lose.

Select every trait you see on the item in Roblox. If you are unsure whether a trait is present, check the trade window again before accepting. Small trait differences are often where unbalanced deals hide.

Should I compare value or income?

Compare both. Value helps you judge whether a player-to-player deal looks balanced as a trade. Income helps you understand how much each side can earn over time after the trade is done.

If value and income point in the same direction, the decision is usually clearer. If they disagree, decide based on your goal: players who want faster earning may prioritize income, while players hunting rarer setups or future trading power may care more about value and mutation quality.

How often are calculator values updated?

SAB Calculator refreshes community value and income data on a regular sync schedule. Most days that keeps the checker close to current trading references, but major Steal a Brainrot updates can still create short gaps while new Brainrots, mutations, or value changes settle.

If a trade feels wrong, re-open the calculator after the next refresh, confirm mutations and traits again, and recheck both value and income before you accept.

Is my Roblox account safe here?

SAB Calculator never asks for your Roblox password, recovery codes, browser cookies, or session tokens. Trade selections are handled in your browser so you can compare an offer without signing into Roblox through this site.

Only complete trades inside the official Roblox trade window. If anyone asks you to share login details, recovery codes, or account cookies in exchange for a “better calculator result,” treat that as a scam and stop.

Are these values official?

No. SAB Calculator is an independent fan-made reference tool. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Roblox Corporation or the Steal a Brainrot developers.

Community values can disagree with what a specific player wants right now. Use the calculator to spot obvious imbalances and missing mutation or trait details, then make the final call yourself.

Where can I check exist counts?

Supply context matters when two Brainrots look close in value. Lower exist counts often signal stronger rarity, but demand and mutations still matter. For tracked exist-count data, use the separate Exist Count tracker at sabexistcount.com.

Exist count is not the same thing as trade value. Use it as supporting context when you are deciding whether a rare-looking offer is actually scarce, then come back to SAB Calculator to check the full trade setup.
