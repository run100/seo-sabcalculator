About this site

About Us

SAB Calculator is an independent Steal a Brainrot trade calculator. Learn how we compare values and how item names, images, and descriptions are used for identification only.

What We Provide

SAB Calculator brings trade comparison tools together in one place. Players can build both sides of a Steal a Brainrot offer, compare community values and income, and factor in mutations and traits that change how a deal looks.

Our calculator presents a clear comparison based on the selected Brainrots and the latest available community estimates. Brainrot pages provide extra context so you are not forced to judge an offer from a single number alone.

Our Goal

Our goal is to make Steal a Brainrot trading information easier to understand and faster to check on desktop and mobile. We focus on clear comparisons, useful context, and a straightforward interface you can use before confirming a trade.

Values, Sources, and Updates

Value, income, mutation, and trait information is compiled from community trading sources and related references. These estimates can move as player interest, availability, and trading activity change.

We work to keep the site accurate and current, but no community value list can guarantee the price another player will accept. Calculator results should be treated as a helpful reference and used together with your own judgment.

Trade Safety

Always review every item and quantity in the final Roblox trade window before accepting. SAB Calculator will never ask for your Roblox password, recovery code, browser cookie, or other account credentials.
